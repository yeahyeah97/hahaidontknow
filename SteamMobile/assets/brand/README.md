# Personal icon geometry

`steam.svg` and `apple.svg` are unchanged paths from Simple Icons 13.21.0:

- https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/13.21.0/icons/steam.svg
- https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/13.21.0/icons/apple.svg

Simple Icons' CC0 dedication is retained in LICENSE.md. It does not grant
trademark rights. The generator scales these paths without hand-redrawing the
logos and renders a 1024px opaque iOS master on navy, with a blue accent and
small white Apple mark at bottom right. No text is placed inside the icon.

This is the user's requested personal build design. Public distribution of
this combined branding has **not** been cleared. Valve's
[branding guidance](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/branding)
restricts prominent third-party use and combining its logo with other marks;
the SVG's software license does not override those restrictions. Do not describe
this app as affiliated with or approved by Valve or Apple. A public release
needs appropriate permission or a separately designed original brand.
